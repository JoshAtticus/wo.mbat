# wo.mbat
wo.mbat is an awesome womp-compatible wasteof.money client for Android, Wear OS*, Chrome OS/GooglebookOS, and iOS built with Jetpack Compose! (and the spiritual successor to wasteof for Android). **Works on Android 6 and later, Wear OS 3 and later, iOS 14.0 and later.**

(I think the name is genius because it's wo.m which is wasteof.money and the mascot for wasteof.money is the wom bat, which is actually a bat and not a wombat but that doesn't matter it's too late to rebrand)

> [!NOTE]
> *The Wear OS app is not standalone and requires a paired phone with Google Play Services Android 11 or later, and the wo.mbat app installed the same way as installed on your watch (for example, if you installed the Wear OS app from Google Play, the phone app must also be installed from Google Play)

## download
You can get it for **Android** from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=wombat.joshattic.us) or GitHub Releases

For **iOS**, as I do not have an Apple Developer Account and do not intend on paying $99 per year for one, you must sideload wo.mbat onto your device. 

There are various ways to do this, for example using Impactor with a normal Apple ID (must be reinstalled every 7 days), paid signing certs (these can be had for a few dollars and last about a year), TrollStore (iOS 14.0-17.0, never has to be reinstalled unless you update your phone which breaks TrollStore) or TrollStore Lite for jailbroken devices.

If this is all new to you, I recommend looking up a tutorial online. https://ios.cfw.guide is great for TrollStore and Jailbreak related stuff.
